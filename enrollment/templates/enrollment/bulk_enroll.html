⚠️ Important Rules
🔴
LRN must be exactly 12 digits — numbers only, no spaces or dashes.
🔴
Gender must be M or F only. Male / Female / male / female are also accepted.
🟠
Date of Birth format: YYYY-MM-DD (example: 2010-05-23). MM/DD/YYYY is also accepted.
🟠
Maximum 500 students per file. Split into multiple files if needed.
🔵
Do not rename, delete, or reorder the column headers in Row 1 of the template.
🔵
Delete the gray sample row (Row 2) before uploading your real data.
