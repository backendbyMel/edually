Create Your Section

Set up your class with the correct grade level and role

Start Here ▼
1

Click "Create Section" from the Sections page

Fill in section name, grade level (7-12), and school details.

2

Choose your class type

Select JHS (Grade 7-10) or SHS (Grade 11-12). This determines the grading weights for subjects.

3

Set your role in this section

Adviser — you handle attendance, school forms, and manually input grades given by subject teachers.
Subject Teacher — you input scores and compute grades for your subject.
Both — you do everything above.

✅
As Adviser: After creating the section, add subjects first — then enroll students. You will manually input the final grades per subject given to you by each subject teacher.
ℹ️
As Subject Teacher: After creating the section, add your subject and mark it as "handled by me." You will input scores per activity and the system computes the quarterly grade automatically.
💡
As Both: You have access to everything. For YOUR subject — scores are auto-computed. For OTHER subjects — you manually input the final grade given by the other teacher.

Add Subjects

Add all subjects taught in your section in the correct order

▼
1

Open your section and go to the Subjects tab

Click on your section from the Sections list, then click the Subjects tab.

2

Add each subject with the correct type and order

The order number determines how subjects appear in school forms like SF9.
Set the correct subject type — this determines the WW/PT/TE grading weights.

3

Check "I am the subject teacher" for your own subjects

This unlocks score input and auto grade computation for that subject.

5

Enroll Students

Search by LRN or name and enroll students into your section

▼
1

Go to the Enrolled Students tab in your section

Click the Enrolled Students tab in your section detail page.

2

Click "Enroll Here" and search for the student

Search by LRN (Learner Reference Number) or student name.

3

If student exists — click Enroll

The student will immediately appear in your enrolled students list.

4

If student does not exist — create the student first

Go to Student List, create the student record with their personal details, then come back to enroll.

🚨
Make sure subjects are added before enrolling students. Some features require subjects to be set up first.
6

Daily Classroom Tasks

What to do every school day depending on your role

▼
🏫 If you are the Adviser:
✓

Record daily attendance

Go to Attendance tab → Record Attendance. Mark each student present, absent, late, or excused.

✓

Input grades from subject teachers

When subject teachers give you their computed final grades, input them per student per subject.

✓

Monitor at-risk students

Check the dashboard for students below 80% attendance or failing grades. Log interventions.

📊 If you are a Subject Teacher:
✓

Create activities for your subject

Go to Subjects tab → click Scores on your subject → Add Activity. Set the type (Graded/Compliance/Recorded) and component (WW/PT/TE).

✓

Input scores per activity

After each activity, go to Input Scores and enter each student's score. Grades are computed automatically.

✓

Ensure minimum DepEd requirements

Make sure you have at least 4 Written Works and 4 Performance Tasks per term. The system alerts you if not.

💡
As both Adviser and Subject Teacher, you do everything above. Your dashboard will show all alerts relevant to both roles.
📋 Your daily checklist:
1

Record attendance for the whole class

Attendance tab → Record Attendance.

2

Input scores for your subject

After your class, go to your subject's activities and input scores.

3

Input grades from other teachers

When other subject teachers submit their grades to you, input them manually for each student.

7

Generate School Forms

Download SF1, SF2, SF9, and SF10 at the end of each term

▼
SF1

School Register (SF1)

List of all enrolled students with personal details. Generate at the start of the school year or any time.

SF2

Daily Attendance Record (SF2)

Monthly attendance summary. Generate at the end of each month. Requires attendance to be recorded daily.

SF9

Report Card (SF9)

Per student report card with final grades. Generate at the end of each term after all grades are inputted.

SF10

Learner's Permanent Academic Record (SF10)

Complete academic history per student. Generate at end of school year.

⚠️
SF9 and SF10 require all grades to be complete before generating. Make sure all subject grades are inputted first.
